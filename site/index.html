◉ le.hweat-penny a pitch in rotations

01

The wheat penny pitch

A security trade-off is not a flag.

It is a price in pennies and a cost in the business need the alternative removes.

02

A vulnerability with no patch is not unactionable.

The action exists — disable the feature, isolate the host, change the flow.

It costs a named business need. “Fix or accept” hides the field that decides: what the fix costs the business.

03

A penny is one verified answer.

isotope
the named invariant — cve_safe, cert-expiry
rotation
one verified subject — a vhost, a step
penny
one rotation

The penny is nominal. Every plane may price its penny differently; the multiple travels, the dollar does not.

Four fields, not one. An “accepted” entry without the alternative and the counter-need is an unowned risk.

05

Two currencies.

The alternative costs pennies to verify and a business need to adopt.

HTTP/2 off: one posture rotation for cve_safe, plus the h2 need. Move to trixie: the migration’s rotations, plus the stability need. Accept: the monitoring rotations, plus the residual risk.

The critic quotes both. It does not collapse the proof into the need, or the need into the proof.

06

The witness.

The posture loop is a witness: local, deterministic, no model spend. cert_collect plus cert_posture is one loop; each step is an isotope, each vhost a rotation.

A passing loop is sterile — it names only the checks it reached. So the floor is stated, not the count.

07

The worked example.

Bookworm had no fixed nginx. “Upgrade” was not the action; the alternatives were:

  1. HTTP/2 off — one rotation to verify the product term cleared. The h2 need is the cost. Taken.
  2. Move to trixie — the migration’s rotations. The stability need is the cost. Open.
  3. Accept — the monitoring rotations. The residual risk is the cost. Open.

The light stayed honest, and the trade-off was named.

08

The rule.

A protection is a spectrum, not a flag.

The trade-off is a position on two axes: the penny cost of proving the alternative, and the business cost of adopting it.

The owner decides the position. The critic records the price and the revisit trigger. It does not decide the need.

09

A wheat penny is still a penny in any plane.

The unit survives the price. The wheat is the need the alternative costs — the thing that grows around the coin, not the coin itself.
